TimeVolt .

An automated university timetable and productivity platform designed to make academic scheduling smarter, faster, and more organized.

OVERVIEW

Solving the scheduling problem.

TimeVolt is a software platform designed around the challenges involved in creating and managing university timetables.

The system is being designed to intelligently organize courses, classrooms, lecturers, departments, and student schedules while reducing common scheduling conflicts.

The project combines a modern frontend with a Python backend and PostgreSQL database, creating a foundation for a complete scheduling platform.
