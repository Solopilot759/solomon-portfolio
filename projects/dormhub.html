DormHub .

A university accommodation management system designed to connect students, hall senators, and hall administrators in one platform.

OVERVIEW

A better campus experience.

DormHub is a project focused on improving the way university accommodation information and management can be handled digitally.

The platform is being designed around multiple user roles, including students, hall senators, and hall administrators.

The project is currently under development, with the interface and system structure being built progressively.
